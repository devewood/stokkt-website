What Stokkt does

Less thinking about food.
Less food in the bin.

Pantry, dinner ideas, the week's plan and the shopping list — in one place, all working off what's actually in your kitchen.

One photo, whole shop

Snap a Woolies, Coles or Aldi receipt and the food goes straight into your pantry with expiry dates and what you paid. Bin bags and batteries are left out.

Use first, every day

Open the app and the food closest to its date is right there, with a dinner idea that uses it. That's the whole trick.

Dinner from what you've got

Fresh ideas written around your pantry, plus real recipes from across the web. Every one shows what you have and the few things you'd need to buy.

The week, in one tap

A week of dinners planned around what goes off first, your household and how you eat. Or use Clear-Out to plan meals that empty the fridge before the next shop.

Cook it, and keep the rest

Step-by-step cook mode with the screen kept awake. When you finish, your pantry updates — and any leftovers go in the fridge with a use-by date.

A list that ticks itself off

Your shopping list builds from your plan and what's running low. Scan the receipt afterwards and everything you bought ticks itself off.

Questions

Good questions.

Is Stokkt free?

Yes. Stokkt is free to download and the free version is yours to keep. Everyone also gets Stokkt Pro on the house for their first 14 days — no card needed.

What happens when my Pro days end?

Nothing is charged, ever, unless you choose to subscribe. You go back to the free version, and everything you've added stays exactly where it is.

Which receipts does it read?

Printed receipts from Woolworths, Coles, Aldi and most other Australian supermarkets. Good light and a flat receipt help. Non-food items like cleaning products are left out automatically.

How does it know how much I saved?

When food leaves your kitchen you tap Used it or Binned it. Stokkt uses the price from your receipt (or a typical Australian price if there isn't one, marked with ~) to add up what you used before it went off, and what was binned.

Is it on Android?

iPhone comes first. Android is on the way — join the list and we'll tell you when it's ready.

Can I share it with my partner or housemate?

Yes. With Pro, two people can share one pantry, meal plan and shopping list. Each of you keeps your own account and profile.
